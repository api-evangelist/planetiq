Under Task Order 1 (TO-1), PlanetiQ will provide a minimum of 4,200 GNSS-RO profiles per day, of which 700 are radio occultation profiles with temporal resolution enhancements, and 500 low-latency TEC tracks per day. The minimum contract value is $28,413,038, with additional "surge" options bringing the total value to $47,342,258 over 2 years.
